    How Much Are the Heat Paying Andrew Wiggins in 2026-27?

    Wiggins earns $30.17m in 2026-27, third at Miami and the last year of his contract. It rises from $28.22m last season.

    His career earnings reach $281.56m across thirteen seasons. The salary is 14.60% of the club’s $206.62m payroll.

    What’s Next for Andrew Wiggins Beyond 2026?

    The contract ends after this season, and the extension already agreed pays $33.83m across 2027-28 and 2028-29 with a player option after it. He turns 32 next February.

    How Long Is Andrew Wiggins’s Contract With the Heat?

    Wiggins is in the last year of a four-year extension worth $109m that he signed with Golden State in 2022 under Bird rights, fully guaranteed. He exercised the player option on this season on June 29, 2026.

    Miami has already agreed a further extension with him, two years and $33.83m from 2027-28, with a player option in 2029-30.

    Why Did the Heat Extend Andrew Wiggins?

    Wiggins is paid $30.17m because a first overall pick who became a starter on a championship team keeps a large number long after the draft.

    • Minnesota gave him $147.71m over five years in 2017, before he had made an All-Star team.
    • He won the championship with Golden State in 2022 and was extended for $109m that summer.
    • He was an All-Star in 2022, the only time in thirteen seasons.
    • He exercised a player option on this season, which is what a player does when the market is thinner than the deal.
    • Miami has already agreed to pay him $33.83m more after it ends.

    He has been paid $281.56m and made one All-Star team.
