    What Is CJ McCollum’s Net Worth in 2026?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $130M
    Published estimate

    McCollum’s net worth is published at around $130m by Celebrity Net Worth. That is well under half the $302.12m he has earned in salary, so the figure is likely to be out of date.

    He played four seasons at Lehigh and Portland took him tenth in 2013. He turned 35 in September.

    Why Is CJ McCollum Worth $21m to the Hawks?

    McCollum is paid $21m because a guard who has scored at this level for a decade still has a price, and one year is what a club will commit to at 35.

    • He was paid $30.67m last season, so this is a third less.
    • He has earned $302.12m in salary, which puts him among the highest paid players of his generation.
    • Portland gave him a four-year extension worth $106.63m in 2016 and another worth $100m in 2019.
    • He turned 35 in September, and clubs stop offering length at that age.
    • The contract carries a 7.5% trade bonus, a clause a player with his service earns.

    His rookie contract at Portland paid him $10.48m across four years, about a thirtieth of what he has earned in total.
