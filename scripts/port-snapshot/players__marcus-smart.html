    How Much Are the Rockets Paying Marcus Smart in 2026-27?

    Smart earns $6.06m in 2026-27, tenth on the Houston payroll and the first year of a new contract. He was paid $20.21m by Washington two seasons ago.

    His career earnings come to $133.25m across thirteen seasons. The salary is 3.03% of Houston’s $200.41m payroll.

    What’s Next for Marcus Smart Beyond 2026?

    One season remains after this one, worth about $6.37m on a player option with a deadline of June 29, 2027. He turns 33 in March, so each contract from here is likely to be a short one.

    How Much Is Marcus Smart’s Contract Worth?

    Smart signed a two-year contract worth $12.43m with Houston on July 10, 2026 using part of the non-taxpayer mid-level exception, fully guaranteed with a player option on the second season.

    He had declined a player option at the Lakers eleven days earlier to reach the market. He becomes an unrestricted free agent in 2028 at 34 if he declines the new option.

    What Is Marcus Smart’s Net Worth?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $53M
    Estimated from career earnings

    Smart’s net worth is estimated at around $53.3m, taken from his $133.25m in career earnings. No outlet publishes a figure for him, and the total excludes whatever Washington paid him to leave in 2025.

    He played two seasons at Oklahoma State and Boston drafted him sixth in 2014. Boston paid him for the first nine years of his career, and the last two contracts are the only ones he has signed on the open market.

    Why Is Marcus Smart Paid $6.06m?

    Smart is paid $6.06m because he is a 32 year old guard whose value has always been on defense, and the mid-level exception is what a club can offer once it is over the cap.

    • He was named Defensive Player of the Year in 2022, the first guard to win it in more than twenty years.
    • He has been selected to three All-Defensive teams across his career.
    • He was paid $20.21m in 2024-25, so this figure is under a third of what he earned two seasons ago.
    • He has missed large parts of the last three seasons through injury, and availability moves the price more than anything else at his age.
    • His career shooting from three has hovered around a third, which is why the number was never higher than it was.

    He spent nine seasons at Boston and has since played for four clubs in four years.
