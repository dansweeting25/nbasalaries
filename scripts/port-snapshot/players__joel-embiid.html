    How Much Is Joel Embiid Paid in 2026-27?

    Embiid earns $57.99m in 2026-27, the largest salary at Philadelphia and the first year of a three-year extension. It rises from $55.22m last season.

    His career earnings come to $379.92m across thirteen seasons, all at Philadelphia. The salary is 27.99% of the club’s $207.19m payroll.

    What’s Next for Joel Embiid Beyond 2026?

    One more season follows this one, then a player option on 2028-29 he can decline by June 29, 2028. Declining it would put him in free agency at 34.

    How Long Is Joel Embiid’s Contract With the 76ers?

    Embiid signed a three-year maximum extension worth $187.87m with Philadelphia in 2024, all of it guaranteed. It starts this season.

    Signing it meant giving up the player option he held on this season. A new player option covers 2028-29, with a June 29, 2028 deadline. He would reach free agency in 2029 at 35.

    What Is Joel Embiid’s Net Worth?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $85M
    Published estimate

    Embiid has a published net worth of around $85m, against $379.92m in career salary. He has substantial endorsement income on top, which a figure like this is meant to include.

    He is from Cameroon and played one season at Kansas. Philadelphia took him third in 2014, three months after he turned 20.

    Why Is Joel Embiid Paid a Maximum Salary?

    Embiid is paid $57.99m because he has signed three maximum contracts in a row with the same club, each one starting before the last had finished.

    • He went third in 2014 and his rookie contract was worth $19.98m.
    • His first extension in 2017 was worth $147.71m.
    • A designated veteran extension in 2021 was worth $213.28m.
    • This one is $187.87m over three years, fully guaranteed.
    • He has earned $379.92m without playing for another club.

    He takes up more than a quarter of what Philadelphia spends.
