    How Much Is Lauri Markkanen’s Net Worth?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $82M
    Estimated from career earnings

    Markkanen’s net worth is estimated at around $81.8m, taken from his $204.5m in career earnings. One outlet puts him at $30m, which sits below a sixth of what he has been paid.

    He is Finnish and played one season at Arizona. Chicago took him seventh in 2017, a month after he turned 20.

    Why Is Lauri Markkanen Paid $46.11m on His Extension?

    Markkanen is paid $46.11m because Utah extended him under Bird rights before he could reach free agency, which is how a club sets the price itself.

    • He went seventh in 2017 and his rookie contract was worth $20.39m.
    • A 2021 sign-and-trade moved him to Cleveland for $67.47m.
    • Utah traded for him in 2022 and extended him two years later.
    • The extension is $195.87m over four years, fully guaranteed.
    • At 7 foot 1 he is the tallest player Utah is paying.

    He is one of two Utah players earning more than $45m.
