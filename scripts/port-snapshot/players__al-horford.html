    How Much Is Al Horford’s Salary at Golden State?

    Horford earns $6.82m in 2026-27, up from $5.69m last season. He’s sixth on the Golden State payroll in his twentieth year, and the four players above him are all at least fourteen years younger.

    He’s been paid about $301.74m across those twenty seasons, which puts him third on this roster for career earnings behind Stephen Curry and Jimmy Butler. His salary this year takes up 3.09% of Golden State’s $220.84m payroll.

    What’s Next for Al Horford Beyond 2026?

    Horford holds a player option on 2027-28 with a deadline of June 29, 2027. He'll be 41 by then, which is past the age almost every big man has stopped, so the decision is likely to be about whether he wants another season at all. Declining it would take him to free agency in the summer of 2027.

    What Does Al Horford’s Contract With the Warriors Include?

    Horford signed with Golden State on July 6, 2026, two years and $13.99m, fully guaranteed, with a player option on the second season.

    The club used Non-Bird rights, which caps what it could offer to a modest raise on his previous salary. He’d been on the taxpayer mid-level exception the year before and declined the option on it in June, so this is the second contract he’s signed in Golden State inside twelve months.

    Al Horford’s Salary As a Percentage of Cap and Payroll

    Horford's $6.82m takes up 4.14% of the $164.96m salary cap and 3.09% of Golden State's $220.84m payroll. The cap is a benchmark, and clubs are allowed to spend past it under certain rules.

    How Much Is Al Horford’s Net Worth Today?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $130M
    Published estimate

    Celebrity Net Worth puts Horford’s net worth at around $130m, updated in June 2026. He’s earned above $26m in seven separate seasons, and his peak stretch ran from 2016-17 to 2022-23 without a single year below $26.50m.

    The gap between that and his $301.74m in career earnings comes mostly from tax, with agent fees and league escrow after it. He’s been paid in Georgia, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, Oklahoma and now California, and the rates vary enough that two identical contracts in different states leave very different amounts behind.

    Why Is Al Horford on $6.82m in 2026-27?

    Horford is on a fraction of his old salary because he’s 40 and plays a reduced number of minutes, and Golden State is paying for what those minutes contain.

    • He shoots from three at a rate that pulls opposing centers away from the basket, which is the specific thing that makes him playable alongside Curry.
    • He defends bigger players without fouling and has done it in postseason series against the best centers in the league, so there’s nothing left to project about the role.
    • He’s played 20 seasons and clubs price age directly, whatever the production says, because availability gets harder to guarantee every year past 35.
    • Golden State signed him with Non-Bird rights, and that mechanism sets a ceiling on the offer before either side starts talking.
    • He has a player option on 2027-28, so he decides whether there’s a twenty-second season rather than waiting to find out.

    He earned $28.93m in 2018-19 and earns less than a quarter of that now.
