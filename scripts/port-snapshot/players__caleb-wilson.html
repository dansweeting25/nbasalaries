    What Is Caleb Wilson’s Salary at Chicago in 2026-27?

    Wilson earns $10.68m in 2026-27, his first season in the league and the sixth highest salary on the Chicago payroll. He was the fourth pick in the draft, and that slot is the whole reason for the figure.

    He turned 20 in July, which makes him the second youngest player on the roster after Noa Essengue. The salary accounts for 6.49% of Chicago’s $164.69m payroll.

    What’s Next for Caleb Wilson Beyond 2026?

    Wilson is under contract to 2029-30 if Chicago exercises both team options, and the first of those falls due in October 2027. He'll be 23 when the deal finishes, young enough that restricted free agency would come at the start of his prime rather than the end.

    What Does Caleb Wilson’s Contract With the Bulls Include?

    Wilson signed his rookie contract on July 3, 2026, four years and $48.52m with $21.90m guaranteed. The third and fourth seasons are team options.

    First-round money is fixed by the rookie scale, so nothing here was negotiated except the standard 120% of the slot figure that almost every pick signs for. Chicago’s committed money runs to the end of next season.

    Wilson’s net worth is estimated at around $4.3m, based on the $10.68m he’s contracted to earn this season. Nobody publishes a figure for a player who hasn’t played a professional game.

    He spent one season at North Carolina before the draft. College players can now earn from name and likeness deals, so a high-profile freshman arrives with money behind him, though none of that appears in the NBA record.

    Why Is Caleb Wilson Paid on the Rookie Scale?

    Wilson is paid $10.68m because he went fourth in the 2026 draft, and the rookie scale sets everything from there.

    • Picks in the top five are the highest paid rookies in the league, and the fourth slot pays roughly three quarters of what the first pick earns.
    • He’s a 6’10” forward who runs the floor and blocks shots, which is the profile clubs take early and develop slowly.
    • He played one college season at 19, so Chicago is paying for a projection rather than a record.
    • Two team options mean the club’s real commitment is $21.90m across two seasons, and it decides on the rest in October 2027.
    • Nothing he does over four years changes the salary, because the scale is fixed by slot.

    He earns more this season than Tre Jones, who has six years in the league.
