    Privacy Policy

    NBA Salaries is an independent research site covering player salaries, contracts and team payrolls. This policy explains what personal data the site collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to visitors in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and the United States.

    The Short Version

    There are no accounts, no newsletter and no paywall. We run no advertising and no analytics. Unless you write to us, the only record of your visit is a server log and a pair of page counters. Nothing on this site is behind a form.

    Who Is Responsible for Your Data

    This site is operated by an independent publisher based in the United Kingdom, acting as the data controller under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can reach us through the contact form with any question about this policy or about data we hold.

    What We Collect

    When You Use the Contact Form

    The contact form asks for your name, your email address, the subject of your enquiry, an optional link to a relevant page, and your message. We collect these so we can read and reply to what you sent. Submissions are stored in the site database and are also sent to the site owner by email. Our lawful basis is legitimate interest: you contacted us and we need the information to respond.

    If your message concerns a correction to our data, we may keep it as part of our editorial record so we can show why a figure changed.

    When You Browse the Site

    Our hosting and our content delivery network record standard server log information, including your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of the request. This keeps the site running, helps diagnose faults and defends against abuse and automated attacks. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in the security and reliability of the service. These logs are not used to build a profile of you.

    What We Do Not Collect

    We run no advertising and no analytics product. We do not ask for or store payment details. We operate no user accounts and no newsletter. We do not ask for your date of birth, we build no profiles of individual readers, and we make no attempt to identify you by name from your browsing.

    Cookies

    The site sets four cookies: one session cookie that is strictly necessary for the site to work, and three belonging to the page builder, two of which simply count visits and page views. There are no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies. Names, purposes and lifetimes are all listed on our Cookie Notice.

    We run no behavioural tracking, so a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal has nothing here to switch off.

    Who Receives Your Data

    A few third parties necessarily receive your IP address, because your browser requests a file from them in order to render the page.

    Cloudflare delivers and protects the site, processing requests on our behalf.

    Google Fonts serves the typefaces used across the site.

    unpkg serves an open source animation library used for page effects.

    cdn.nba.com serves official team logos and player headshots.

    These providers receive the technical information any web server receives when it serves a file. They are not given your contact form submission, and we pass them nothing about you beyond the request itself. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

    How Long Things Are Kept

    Contact form submissions are kept for as long as they are useful to answer your enquiry and to maintain our correction record, and are reviewed periodically. Server and security logs are retained on a short rolling basis by our hosting provider and our content delivery network. Cookies expire as set out in the Cookie Notice.

    Where Your Data Is Held

    The site is hosted in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area. Some of the providers listed above operate globally and may process request data in the United States or elsewhere. Where personal data moves outside the UK or EEA, that transfer relies on the safeguards those providers have in place, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, certification under the EU-US and UK-US Data Privacy Framework.

    Your Rights in the UK and the EEA

    You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on legitimate interest you may object, and we will stop unless we have compelling grounds not to. Exercising any of these is free and we will respond within one month.

    Use the contact form to make a request. In practice the only personal data we are likely to hold about you is a message you sent us, since we do not track readers.

    If you are unhappy with how we handle a request you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to your local supervisory authority in the EEA.

    Your Rights in the United States

    If you are in California, you have the right to know what personal information is collected and for what purpose, to request its deletion, to correct it, and not to be treated differently for exercising those rights. Residents of other states with comparable privacy laws have similar rights.

    We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as that term is defined under California law. To make a request, use the contact form.

    The categories we handle are limited: identifiers such as IP address; internet activity such as the pages you request; and, only if you write to us, the name, email address and message you chose to send.

    Children

    This site publishes sports business research and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal data through the contact form, tell us and we will delete it.

    Our Data on Players and Teams

    The salary, contract and payroll figures published here concern the professional and contractual arrangements of public figures, compiled from published sources such as official league announcements and established salary databases. Our methodology page explains how we source and check them.

    If you are a player, an agent or a club representative and believe a published figure about you is inaccurate, tell us through the contact form and we will review it promptly.

    Changes to This Policy

    This page describes the site as it is today. We update it when what we do changes, and change the date at the top.
