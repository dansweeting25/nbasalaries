    Williamson earns $42.17m in 2026-27, the largest salary at New Orleans. It rises from $39.45m last season.

    His career earnings reach $196.61m across eight seasons, all of them at New Orleans. The salary is 21.46% of the club’s $196.53m payroll.

    How Much Is Zion Williamson’s Contract Worth?

    Williamson is in the fourth year of a five-year extension worth $197.23m that he signed with New Orleans in 2022 under Bird rights.

    Several seasons of it started out partly unprotected, with guarantee dates running through the season. He becomes an unrestricted free agent in 2028 at 27.

    How Much Is Zion Williamson’s Net Worth Today?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $70M
    Published estimate

    Williamson’s net worth is published at around $70m by Celebrity Net Worth, against $196.61m in career salary. He signed one of the largest shoe deals ever given to a rookie, which sits outside the salary figure.

    He played one season at Duke and New Orleans took him first in 2019, two weeks before he turned 19.

    Why Did the Pelicans Extend Zion Williamson?

    Williamson is paid $42.17m because he was the first pick and scores at the rim more efficiently than almost anyone, and the contract was written to protect the club against how often he plays.

    • The extension would have paid $231m instead of $193m had he made an All-NBA team.
    • Two of its seasons began only partly guaranteed, with dates running into January.
    • His pay went from $13.53m to $34.01m when it started.
    • He missed entire seasons early in his career.
    • He is 6’6″ and 284 pounds, the heaviest player at New Orleans by some way.

    He has never played a professional game for any club but New Orleans.
