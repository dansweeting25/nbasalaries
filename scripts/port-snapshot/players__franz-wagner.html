    Wagner earns $41.75m in 2026-27, the largest salary at Orlando and the second year of a five-year extension. It rises from $38.66m last season.

    His career earnings come to $103.2m across six seasons, all at Orlando. The salary is 19.90% of the club’s $209.87m payroll.

    How Much Is Franz Wagner’s Contract Worth at Orlando?

    Wagner signed a five-year extension worth $224.24m with Orlando in 2024, all of it guaranteed.

    It was written with clauses that would have raised it to 27.5% or 30% of the cap on certain awards. Neither triggered, so it pays the base rate. He reaches free agency in 2030 at 28.

    Why Is Franz Wagner on a Rookie Extension?

    Wagner is paid $41.75m because Orlando extended him under the designated rookie rules, which set the money as a share of the cap.

    • He went eighth in 2021 and his rookie contract was worth $22.78m.
    • The club exercised both options on it, in 2022 and 2023.
    • The extension is $224.24m over five years, fully guaranteed.
    • Clauses that would have raised it to 27.5% or 30% of the cap did not trigger.
    • His salary went from $7.01m to $38.66m in one year.

    He has never been paid by another club.
