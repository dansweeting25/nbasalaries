    What Is Josh Hart’s Salary With the Knicks?

    Hart earns $20.92m in 2026-27, fifth at New York and the third year of a four-year extension. It rises from $19.47m last season.

    His career earnings come to $104.93m across ten seasons. The salary is 9.47% of the club’s $220.86m payroll.

    What’s Next for Josh Hart Beyond 2026?

    A club option on 2027-28 follows this season. New York decides that one, and declining it would put him in free agency at 32.

    How Much Is Josh Hart Worth?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $30M
    Published estimate

    Hart has a published net worth of around $30m, against $104.93m in career salary. He earned under $9m across his first four seasons, so most of it has arrived since 2021.

    He played four seasons at Villanova and went 30th in the 2017 draft, four months after he turned 22.

    Why Did the Knicks Extend Josh Hart?

    Hart is paid $20.92m because New York extended him a year before he could reach free agency, which is what a club does when it wants to set the price itself.

    • He went 30th in 2017, the last pick of the first round, on a rookie deal worth $8.47m.
    • New Orleans signed him in 2021 for $37.92m and traded him inside two years.
    • New York traded for him in February 2023 and extended him that October.
    • Of the $80.92m on the extension, $58.54m is guaranteed.
    • The last season is a club option, so New York decides whether it happens.

    He is one of three Villanova players New York is paying this season.
