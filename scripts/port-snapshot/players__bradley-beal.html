    What Is Bradley Beal’s Contract With the Clippers?

    Beal signed a two-year contract worth $13.17m with the Clippers on August 13, 2026 under Non-Bird rights, with $3.21m guaranteed at signing and the rest locking in five days later.

    How Much Is Bradley Beal Worth?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $100M
    Published estimate

    Published estimates put Beal’s net worth at around $100m, against $329.86m in career earnings. He has also been paid by Phoenix since it released him, which no public figure captures.

    Washington took him third in 2012 out of Florida. He was an All-Star three times and spent eleven seasons at the club before a trade to Phoenix in 2023.

    Why Do the Clippers Pay Bradley Beal $6.42m?

    Beal is paid $6.42m because a club that has already been released from a maximum contract has to sign again at whatever the market offers, and this is it.

    • He signed a five-year maximum worth $251.02m at Washington in 2022 and was released before it ran out.
    • He was paid $50.2m in 2024-25, so this figure is about an eighth of that.
    • He averaged above 30 points a game in 2020-21, one of the highest scoring seasons of the last decade.
    • Injuries have kept him under 60 games in most recent seasons.
    • Only $3.21m was guaranteed when he signed, which is what a club risks on a player of 33 with that record.

    The maximum contract he signed in 2022 was worth more than the whole Clippers payroll for this season.
