    Why Is Zach LaVine Paid a Maximum Salary?

    LaVine is paid $48.97m because Chicago signed him to a maximum contract in 2022, and a trade carries that contract across untouched.

    • He went 13th in 2014 and his rookie contract was worth $9.65m.
    • Chicago signed him for $78m using cap space in 2018.
    • That deal paid a flat $19.5m for four straight seasons.
    • The maximum contract is $215.16m over five years.
    • Its 15% trade bonus paid him $3m when Sacramento acquired him.

    He exercised the option on this season instead of testing free agency at 31.
