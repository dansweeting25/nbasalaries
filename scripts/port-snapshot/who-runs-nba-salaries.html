    Who Runs NBA Salaries

    NBA Salaries is an independent site run by Dan Sweeting, a UK-based digital marketing specialist and basketball fan. It has no connection to the NBA, the players' union or any team.
    Dan Sweeting
    How the site works
    Independence
    Questions

    Founded and Edited by Dan Sweeting

    Dan started NBA Salaries in 2026 and runs it in his spare time. He decides what each page covers, which sources a figure can come from, and when a page needs updating.

    He also runs F1 Salaries, a sister site on Formula 1 pay. Most NBA contracts are public, so the job here is different: keeping every player page and all 30 team payrolls accurate as trades, waivers, extensions and option decisions land through the season.

    How the Site Works

    Sources

    Public Records Only

    Contract terms come from team announcements, the NBA's transaction log and established trackers such as Basketball-Reference and Hoops Rumors. Nothing comes from private documents.

    Checks

    Totals That Add Up

    Team payrolls are rebuilt from each player's contract, so a team page, its roster table and every player page show the same figure.

    Updates

    Moves Logged as They Happen

    When a player is traded, waived or extended, his page, both team pages and any teammate page that quotes the old payroll are updated together.

    Independent and Free to Read

    No Ties to the League

    NBA Salaries is not affiliated with the NBA, the NBPA, any team, agency or sportsbook, and no one can pay to change a figure.

    Free for Everyone

    There is no paywall and no sponsored content. If advertising is added, advertisers will have no say over what is published.

    Corrections Welcome

    If a figure looks wrong, send the page and a source. Every correction is checked and the page is updated.
