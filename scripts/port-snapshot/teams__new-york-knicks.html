    What Does the New York Knicks’ Payroll Look Like in 2026-27?

    New York Knicks salaries add up to a cap payroll of $220.86 million for 2026–27, $55.90 million above the $164.96 million salary cap.

    Karl-Anthony Towns tops the payroll at $57.08 million, ahead of OG Anunoby on $42.50 million and Jalen Brunson on $37.74 million, together about 62.2% of the roster total. Tax salary is $20.43 million above the $200.43 million luxury-tax line and apron salary is $11.85 million over the $209.02 million first apron, $0.82 million short of the $221.69 million second apron.

    The New York Knicks standard roster accounts for $220.86M. Add $0.00M in dead cap to reach $220.86M before cap holds. Holds are separate accounting charges, not signed salary payments.

    What Does Going Over the Tax Line Mean?

    The luxury tax is an additional team expense. It rises in bands as tax salary moves above the threshold; teams with a recent history of paying tax can face higher repeater rates. The final bill depends on the season-end calculation.

    Why the Aprons Matter

    Aprons affect how a team can build its roster, including certain exceptions and trades. Being below an apron does not automatically create cap space, and a payroll figure alone does not establish whether a hard cap has been triggered.
