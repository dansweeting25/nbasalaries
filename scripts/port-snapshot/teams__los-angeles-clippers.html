    How Much Is the LA Clippers’ Payroll in 2026-27?

    LA Clippers salaries put $183.07 million on the books for 2026–27, which is $18.11 million above the $164.96 million salary cap. That figure includes $2.15 million in dead cap for Johni Broome, waived in September, on top of a standard roster of $180.92 million.

    Darius Garland is the largest salary at $42.17 million, followed by Brandon Ingram on $40.00 million and Max Strus on $16.66 million, about 54.6% of the roster total between them. Their tax salary is $17.36 million below the $200.43 million luxury-tax line and apron salary sits $25.95 million under the $209.02 million first apron.

    The LA Clippers standard roster accounts for $180.92M. Add $2.15M in dead cap to reach $183.07M before cap holds. Holds are separate accounting charges, not signed salary payments.

    What Does Going Over the Tax Line Mean?

    The luxury tax is an additional team expense. It rises in bands as tax salary moves above the threshold; teams with a recent history of paying tax can face higher repeater rates. The final bill depends on the season-end calculation.

    Why the Aprons Matter

    Aprons affect how a team can build its roster, including certain exceptions and trades. Being below an apron does not automatically create cap space, and a payroll figure alone does not establish whether a hard cap has been triggered.

    The Payroll Over Time

    Across the four completed seasons shown, cap payroll ranged from $170.72M to $200.06M. The current roster puts 40.25% of active cap payroll into its three largest contracts.

    What Contracts Do the Clippers Have on the Books?

    The contracts the Clippers have on the books turn over quickly. Five deals end after this season, Max Strus, Derrick Jones Jr., Brook Lopez and Gradey Dick among them, which takes a large share of the payroll off at once.

    Garland’s designated rookie extension, worth $197.23 million, runs one year longer, to 2027–28, as does Brandon Ingram’s deal, which has a player option for that season. Ingram and Dick arrived from Toronto in September in the trade that sent Kawhi Leonard to the Raptors. After that the only commitments reaching 2029–30 are the contracts of Keaton Wagler, Kobe Sanders and Baba Miller, all signed this year.
