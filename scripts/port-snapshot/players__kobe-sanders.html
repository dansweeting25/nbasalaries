    Sanders earns $2.62m in 2026-27, fourteenth on the Clippers payroll and the first standard salary of his career.

    His rookie season carried no published figure, so this is the whole of his published career earnings. The salary is 1.45% of the club’s $180.92m payroll.

    What’s Next for Kobe Sanders Beyond 2026?

    Three seasons remain after this one, worth about $8.66m in total with about $2.75m of it guaranteed. He reaches free agency in 2030 at 28 if the club keeps him.

    How Much Is Kobe Sanders’s Net Worth Today?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $1M
    Estimated from career earnings

    Sanders’s net worth is estimated at around $1.05m, taken from the $2.62m he is contracted to earn this season. His first season published no salary.

    He spent four years at Cal Poly and a fifth at Nevada, and the Clippers took him 50th in 2025. He turned 23 three weeks before the draft.

    Why Is Kobe Sanders Paid $2.62m?

    Sanders is paid $2.62m because a second-round pick has no pay scale, and the club that drafted him decided what to guarantee after a year of watching him.

    • He went 50th in 2025, so his first deal was a two-way contract with $85.3k protected.
    • He is 6’8″ and plays as a guard, which is a size clubs will always try.
    • Less than half the four years is guaranteed.
    • He played five college seasons, so he arrived in the NBA at 23.
    • The Clippers already pay Ingram, Jones, Miller and Strus ahead of him on the wing.

    He has signed three contracts with the Clippers in fourteen months.
