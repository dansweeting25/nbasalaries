    How Much Is Amen Thompson Paid in 2026-27?

    Thompson earns $12.26m in 2026-27, seventh on the Houston payroll and the last year of his rookie contract. It rises from $9.69m last season.

    His career earnings come to $40.01m across four seasons, all at Houston. The salary is 6.12% of the club’s $200.41m payroll.

    What’s Next for Amen Thompson Beyond 2026?

    The extension takes over in 2027-28 and pays about $41.6m a season for five years, all guaranteed. He reaches unrestricted free agency in 2032 at 29, which is the age at which a guard signs his largest contract.

    When Does Amen Thompson’s Contract Expire?

    Thompson is in the final season of the four-year rookie contract worth $40.01m he signed on July 3, 2023. Houston exercised both club options on it early, in October 2024 and October 2025.

    He agreed a five-year extension worth $208m on September 3, 2026, fully guaranteed with a 10% trade bonus. It starts in 2027-28 and runs to 2031-32, when he will be 29.

    Amen Thompson’s Salary As a Percentage of Cap and Payroll

    Thompson's $12.26m takes up 7.43% of the $164.96m salary cap and 6.12% of Houston's $200.41m payroll. The cap is a benchmark, and clubs are allowed to spend past it under certain rules.

    How Much Is Amen Thompson’s Net Worth Today?

    Estimated Net Worth
    $16M
    Estimated from career earnings

    Thompson’s net worth is estimated at around $16m, taken from his $40.01m in career earnings. No outlet publishes a figure for him.

    He never went to college. He and his twin brother Ausar played for Overtime Elite, a paid league for teenagers, and both were taken in the top five of the 2023 draft. Ausar went fifth to Detroit, one pick after Amen.

    Why Is Amen Thompson Paid on the Rookie Scale?

    Thompson is paid $12.26m this season because the rookie scale sets the figure for the fourth pick in the draft, and the $208m he agreed in September is what Houston thinks he is actually worth.

    • He was named to the All-Defensive First Team in 2025 at 22, which almost nobody does that early.
    • He is 6’7″ and guards point guards, which is the reason his defensive value is priced so highly.
    • His shooting from distance is the weakest part of his game, and it is the one thing holding the number down.
    • The extension pays him more than four times his current salary from next season.
    • Houston exercised both club options a day before each deadline, which tells you how the club rates him.

    His $208m extension is the largest commitment on the Houston books.
