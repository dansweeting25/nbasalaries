    How Much Are NBA Players Paid?

    NBA players earn an average of $11.28M in 2026–27, but the typical player makes far less: the median salary is $5.30M. Pay ranges from $678,882 on a two-way contract to $62.59M for Stephen Curry, and the league’s 519 players earn $5.86B combined.

    Salaries are shaped by the collective bargaining agreement. First-round picks are paid on a rookie scale, minimum salaries rise with years of service, and the largest contracts are limited to a share of the salary cap: 25%, 30%, or 35% depending on experience.
