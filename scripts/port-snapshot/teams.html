    How Much Do NBA Teams Spend?

    The 30 NBA teams carry $5.92B in cap payroll for 2026–27, an average of $197.48M per team and a median of $198.69M. The salary cap is $164,961,000, the luxury-tax line sits at $200,428,000, and the two aprons follow at $209,015,000 and $221,686,000.

    Cap payroll counts the active roster plus dead money. Teams can spend past the cap using exceptions such as Bird rights, but every dollar above the tax line carries a penalty, and the aprons restrict trades, signings and draft picks. 13 teams are above the tax line this season, while 2 are still under the cap.
